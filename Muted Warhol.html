What this gets right

  • Dark mode compatible. The original Warhol needs cream; this runs on black.
  • Muted palette coexists with photography — travel images stay the visual hero, not the UI.
  • Keeps the Warhol layout DNA: hard outlines, bold Archivo type, repeating-grid story rings.
  • Muted pink still owns brand identity; muted gold still pops on ratings / sales without blinding you.
  • Closer to Instagram-calm than pure Warhol, but still visually ownable.
  • WCAG AA accessible on all primary text/surface pairs (tested).

What you lose

  • You lose the pure pop impact — banana yellow and Marilyn pink are iconic precisely because they're loud.
  • The muted palette can read generic if you don't commit to the hard-outline / bold-type signature.
  • Story rings with 2px outline borders (vs gradient fills) are less Instagram-native.
  • If you want "ownable color," this trades saturation for accessibility — a conscious tradeoff.
